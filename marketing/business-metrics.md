## 1. Market Size Calculation

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **Serbian weddings annually:** 35,000
- **Average digital invitation spend potential:** €59 (Standard tier benchmark)
- **TAM = 35,000 × €59 = €2,065,000**

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
Digital-savvy couples likely to consider online invitations:
- **Target demographic:** Ages 23-35 with Instagram presence
- **Estimated % of market:** 60% (urban, tech-savvy couples)
- **SAM = 35,000 × 60% × €59 = €1,239,000**
- **SAM in customers:** 21,000 couples/year

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
Realistic first-year market capture:
- **Year 1 conservative target:** 1.5% of SAM = 315 customers
- **Year 1 realistic target:** 3.0% of SAM = 630 customers
- **Year 1 optimistic target:** 5.0% of SAM = 1,050 customers

**Revenue Potential (Year 1):**
| Scenario | Market Share | Customers | Avg Price | Revenue |
|----------|--------------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Conservative | 1.5% | 315 | €59 | €18,585 |
| Realistic | 3.0% | 630 | €59 | €37,170 |
| Optimistic | 5.0% | 1,050 | €59 | €61,950 |

### Market Validation
- **Competitor pricing:** sajtzavencanje.rs at €59-109 validates premium digital market
- **Print displacement:** €50-200 for 100 cards makes €59 digital highly competitive
- **Wedding budget context:** €59 is 0.4-1.2% of average wedding spend (€5,000-15,000)
- **Instagram penetration:** 70%+ of target demographic active on platform
